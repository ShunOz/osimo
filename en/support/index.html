Support

OSIMO is an iPhone alarm app for waking up with photos and voices of your oshi (the person or character you support).

Contact

Email: shunoz.contact@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, what happened, and the steps you took. Please do not send photos, videos or personal information unless we ask for them. A reply may take a few days.

If an alarm doesn't ring

  • In OSIMO, check that the alarm is on, and check its days and the holiday-off setting.
  • In the iPhone Settings app, open OSIMO and make sure alarms and notifications are allowed.
  • Check the volume. OSIMO uses the iPhone's alarm system (AlarmKit), so alarms ring even in Silent mode or a Focus.
  • OSIMO schedules alarms a few days ahead and reschedules them when you open the app. If you haven't opened it for a while, please open it once.

Turning off Sleep mode

Tap "End Sleep mode" on the Home screen. Your regular alarms are scheduled again afterwards.

Purchases, cancellation and restore

  • You can view or cancel your OSIMO PLUS subscription in the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.
  • If a purchase doesn't appear (for example, on a new iPhone), tap Settings → Restore Purchases in the app while signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • The free trial length and price are shown on Apple's purchase sheet. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Moving to a new iPhone and deleting data

When iCloud is available, your oshi details, photos, alarm settings and records sync to your own iCloud and carry over to a new iPhone signed in with the same Apple Account. Videos and alarm voice files stay on the device only, so please import them again on the new iPhone. For deletion, see the Privacy Policy.
