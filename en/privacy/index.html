Privacy Policy

Effective: November 8, 2026 · Operator: Shun Ozawa · Contact: shunoz.contact@gmail.com

OSIMO ("the App") uses the oshi details, photos, videos, voices, alarms and events you set up to provide alarms, notifications, widgets and related features. The operator does not run any server that collects your information.

1. Information the App handles

The App uses the following information that you enter or choose, only to provide its features:

  • Your fave's name, birthday, member color, and notification sender name and messages
  • Photos and videos you choose, and voice clips you cut from them
  • Alarms, events, and records such as the time you woke up
  • TimeTree public calendar URLs you register, and events you import through the share feature

The App only reads the photos and videos you pick. It does not read your whole photo library.

2. Storage and iCloud

Your information is stored on your device. When iCloud is available, your fave's details, photos, alarm settings, events and records sync to your own iCloud through Apple's CloudKit. Videos and alarm voice files stay on your device and are not synced to iCloud.

The operator never receives or views this information.

3. Third parties, analytics and advertising

The App shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Your information is never shared with or sold to third parties.

When you open a TimeTree calendar from the App, the page is shown in an in-app browser and TimeTree's privacy policy applies.

4. Notifications

Alarms and notifications are scheduled and shown on your device, based on your settings and the permissions you grant.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple. The operator does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to purchases.

6. Deleting your information

  • You can delete faves, alarms, events and other items individually in the App.
  • Deleting the App removes the information stored on your device, including videos and voice files.
  • To delete data synced to iCloud, go to the iPhone Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

7. Children

The App does not send personal information to the operator, regardless of the user's age.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.
